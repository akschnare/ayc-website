History of Melville Island & AYC

Armdale Yacht Club’s property can be tied to events dating back at least to 1732. Since the arrival of Europeans the island has been a family estate, hospital, quarantine station, military prison, prisoner-of-war camp, recruit training station for the British Foreign Legion, ammunition depot — and a yacht club.

French prisoners arrived from 1808 during the Napoleonic Wars, and American prisoners during the War of 1812. The Canadian military took control of the four-acre island in 1905; the stone prison held German prisoners of war in the First World War. The military left in 1945, and AYC’s founders negotiated a long-term lease with the Department of National Defence. Five buildings from that period remain — one is our clubhouse, built in 1808 on the island’s highest point.
